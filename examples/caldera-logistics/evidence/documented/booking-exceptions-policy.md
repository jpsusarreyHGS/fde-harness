# Booking exceptions — policy

Issued by Operations, January 2025.

## Principle

Every deviation from a confirmed booking is recorded in the TMS against the
booking reference, with a reason code, before the deviation is actioned.

## Reason codes

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| EX01 | Customer-requested change |
| EX02 | Carrier unable to meet window |
| EX03 | Incomplete booking data |
| EX04 | Vehicle or driver unavailable |
| EX05 | Restricted or hazardous load |

## Communication

All communication with carriers and drivers relating to a booking must take
place through the TMS messaging module so that it is retained against the
booking.

## Authority

Changes affecting price or delivery date require supervisor approval.
