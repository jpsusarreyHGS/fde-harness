# Call — Priya Raman, Director of Claims Operations, 11 September

Present: Priya Raman (Northwind), JP (HGS). 40 minutes.

Priya opened with what she wants: "a dashboard so I can see where the
backlog is."

Asked what she would do differently if she had it: long pause. "I suppose
I'd know which broker was sending us rubbish."

On volume: "We take about two hundred FNOLs a week. Ana does most of the
motor ones. I genuinely don't know how she does it."

On the SOP: "There is one, yes. It's on the intranet. I'd guess it's a few
years old — nobody's asked me to sign a new one since I took the role."

On what goes wrong: "Complaints. If we decline something we shouldn't have,
that's an ombudsman case and it costs us about four thousand pounds before
anyone has even looked at the merits. So the team are cautious, and I'd
rather they were."

Asked about the queue of things waiting for her: "Is there one? I get
emails. I deal with them when I can."

On fleet: "Fleet is a different animal. Different wordings, different
limits. We only have two people signed off on it."

On the broker spreadsheet: "That is Maureen's. She built it. I think she
retired in — no, she's still here, two days a week."

On measurement: "We report acknowledgement time to the board. It's green.
I'm not sure it means anything."
