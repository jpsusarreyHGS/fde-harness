# FNOL triage — standard operating procedure

Document owner: Claims Operations. Version 4.2, last reviewed March 2024.

## Purpose

Ensure every first notification of loss is acknowledged within 4 business
hours and assigned to an adjuster within one business day.

## Procedure

1. Retrieve the notification from the FNOL shared mailbox.
2. Validate the policy number against ClaimCenter. If no match, return the
   notification to the broker requesting a valid policy reference.
3. Confirm the loss date falls within the cover period. If it does not,
   decline the notification and issue the standard decline letter.
4. Create the claim record in ClaimCenter.
5. Assign to the adjuster pool by line of business.
6. Acknowledge to the broker within 4 business hours.

## Escalation

Claims exceeding £50,000 estimated value are escalated to a senior adjuster
before creation.

## Service levels

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | 4 business hours |
| Assignment | 1 business day |
| Rejection for invalid policy | Same day |
