# Prior authorisation — documented workflow

Approved by Clinical Operations, June 2023. Review due June 2025 (overdue).

## Intake channels

All prior-authorisation requests arrive through the Epic provider portal.

## Steps

1. Request appears in the Epic work queue.
2. Coordinator verifies member eligibility in Epic.
3. Coordinator verifies the procedure is covered under the member's plan.
4. Complete requests are routed to a clinical reviewer.
5. Incomplete requests are returned to the submitting provider through the
   portal with a standard reason code.

## Turnaround

| Priority | Target |
|---|---|
| Routine | 5 business days |
| Expedited | 72 hours |

Expedited status is set by the submitting provider in the portal.

## Note

Fax intake was retired in 2022.
