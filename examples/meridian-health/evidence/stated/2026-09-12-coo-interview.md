# Interview — Dr Hakim Osei, COO Shared Services, 12 September

On the ask: "We want to cut prior-auth turnaround. The board has seen the
numbers and they are not good."

On the current process: "It all comes through Epic. We moved off fax years
ago."
(Noted. The shadowing session two days earlier began with the fax queue.)

On volume: "Around six hundred a week across all modalities. Imaging is the
biggest chunk."

On the team: "Two coordinators. It should be three. We've had a vacancy
open since February."

On what a delay costs: "Clinically, it varies. Commercially, a delayed
authorisation is a procedure that happens somewhere else, and that revenue
does not come back."

On the works council: "They'll want to be involved. They were burnt by a
scheduling system a few years back that started scoring people. Go and talk
to them before you do anything."

On the Access database: "The what?"

On expedited requests: "The provider flags it in the portal. That's the
mechanism."
Asked whether coordinators ever escalate something the provider has not
flagged: "They shouldn't. But I'd rather they did than not."
